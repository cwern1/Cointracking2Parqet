Crypto sync for Parqet

Cointracking2Parqet

Import every crypto transaction from CoinTracking into a Parqet portfolio. Runs in your browser. Your CoinTracking secret never leaves it. Requests pass through a small relay that stores and logs nothing.

2 CoinTracking

Create a key with read access only under CoinTracking → API. The secret signs each request here in your browser and is never sent anywhere.
